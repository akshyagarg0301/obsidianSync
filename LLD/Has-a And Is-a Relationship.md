has-a relationship shows a link between 2 objects.
1. House has rooms
2. Library has books
3. School has students
This is called Association.

Association has two parts
1. Aggregation:- Weak Relationship. Existence of one object is not dependent on other. For example:- library has books. Both can exist individually.
2. Composition:- Strong Relationship. Existence of one object is dependent on other. For example:- House has rooms. Rooms cannot exist without house.
